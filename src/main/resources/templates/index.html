How it works

1. Report

Describe the problem, pick a category, add the location and an optional photo.

2. Review

A municipal officer reviews the complaint and sets its priority.

3. Assign & fix

The complaint is assigned to a field worker who updates progress.

4. Verify

The officer verifies the work, closes the case, and you can leave feedback.

What can you report?

Potholes & damaged roads Garbage accumulation Broken streetlights Water leakage Drainage issues Other civic problems
