Welcome to CivicTrack, Name!

Role

  1. Report an issue
    Choose Report an Issue, describe the problem, drop a pin on the map and add a photo.
  2. Keep your reference number
    Every complaint gets one (like CT-AB12-CD34). Anyone can track it on the Track a complaint page.
  3. Follow the progress
    The bell tells you about every update. You can comment, withdraw before assignment, or reopen within 7 days of closing.
  4. Rate the result
    When work is done, leave feedback so the city can improve.
  1. Review new complaints
    Open a complaint, start the review, set its priority or reject it with a reason.
  2. Assign the work
    Pick a field worker (least busy first) and add instructions - the worker is notified.
  3. Watch the map and overdue work
    The Map shows every pin by status; red marks overdue complaints.
  4. Verify and close
    When a worker marks a job completed you are notified: verify it, close it, or send it back.
  1. Your assignments
    New jobs appear in My Assignments and the bell, with the authority's instructions.
  2. Find the place
    Use the Map view and the Get directions link on each complaint.
  3. Update the work
    Start work, add progress notes (tick "I need the authority's attention" for problems) and mark it completed.
  4. See your results
    My stats shows your completed jobs, speed and what citizens said.
  1. Manage people
    In Users create staff accounts, edit roles and emails, reset passwords and see each person's activity.
  2. Announce and organise
    Under More: publish announcements, manage categories and read the audit log.
  3. Watch the numbers
    This overview, the Workers page and the satisfaction summary show how the city is doing. You have read-only access to complaints.

Tip: use the moon/sun button for dark mode. You can open this guide again from My profile.
