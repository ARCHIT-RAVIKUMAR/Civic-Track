Help & FAQ

Everything you need to use CivicTrack.

How it works

  1. Report - a citizen describes the problem, picks a category, adds the location (and optionally a map pin and photo).
  2. Review - a municipal officer reviews it and sets its priority.
  3. Assign & fix - the officer assigns it to a field worker, who updates the progress.
  4. Verify - the worker marks it completed, the officer verifies and closes it, and the citizen can leave feedback.

What the statuses mean

SubmittedReceived and waiting for an officer.
Under ReviewAn officer is checking it.
AssignedA field worker has been chosen.
In ProgressWork has started.
ResolvedThe worker says it is done; waiting for the officer to verify.
ClosedVerified and finished.
RejectedNot valid or a duplicate; the reason is shown on the complaint.
WithdrawnCancelled by the citizen before it was assigned.

Privacy

  • We store your name, email, the complaints you submit (including any photo and map location) and your comments.
  • Your password is stored only as a secure hash. Administrators cannot see it.
  • Account events such as logins are recorded for security and are visible to administrators only. IP addresses are not stored.
  • The public tracking page shows only the status and dates, never names or descriptions.
  • This is a college demonstration project: please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Need more help?

For account problems, contact the system administrator of your municipality. For a problem with the site itself, report it to the project team.
